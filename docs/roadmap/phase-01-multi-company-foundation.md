# Historical Phase 01: Multi-Company Foundation

**Status:** Superseded by the Workspace, Client and Service foundation
**Reason:** The original phase incorrectly used Company for ordinary agency clients.

## Preserved value

The implemented tenant isolation, permissions, audit events, brand profiles, knowledge records and context-switching foundations remain useful. They must be adapted rather than discarded.

## Corrected direction

- Workspace/Tenant is the SaaS isolation boundary.
- Client represents an agency customer.
- Service Engagement represents purchased work.
- Client Brain owns approved client and brand context.
- Company becomes an optional Operating Context for AZMIN's own brands, employers, partners or joint ventures.
- Existing Company-linked data must be migrated with reconciliation and rollback evidence.

The active plan is maintained in [Implementation Roadmap](implementation-roadmap.md), and the decision is recorded in [ADR 0004](../decisions/0004-client-service-engagement-ownership.md).
