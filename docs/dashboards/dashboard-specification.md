# Dashboard and Reporting Specification

## Principles

- Answer a decision, not merely display data.
- Show exceptions and required actions first.
- Use plain-language labels and consistent statuses.
- Permit drill-down from a chart to its records.
- Always show active company and date range.
- Use a number/list when it communicates better than a chart.

## AZMIN Owner dashboard

Answers: **What needs attention and how is the business doing?**

- Daily AI briefing and approval inbox
- Leads requiring follow-up
- Project/task risks
- Outstanding payments and commissions
- Renewals due in 7, 30 and 90 days
- Content/publishing exceptions
- Deployment, automation and monitoring failures
- Revenue, cost, profit and AI/provider costs

Recommended visuals: revenue/profit trend, sales funnel, profit by service,
project status, renewal timeline, content pipeline and automation success rate.

## Company dashboard

Shows only the active company's services, agreements, leads, projects, tasks,
approvals, content, campaigns, invoices, renewals, infrastructure health, agent
activity and cost.

## Platform Admin dashboard

Reserved for future SaaS platform operators:

- Tenants, trials, subscriptions and suspended accounts
- Users, storage, AI and automation usage
- Failed jobs and platform health
- Billing/entitlement problems
- Security events and support requests
- Feature flags and announcements

Support access to customer data must be authorized, time-bound and audited.

## Metric definitions

Every metric documents its business definition, formula, source records, timezone,
filters, owner and limitations. Charts link to filtered records so totals can be
verified.
